FAQ

Questions people ask us

Is this just another chatbot?

No. It's designed to proactively organize work, recognize pending commitments, prepare actions, and support you throughout the day — rather than wait for you to ask.

Will it send messages without permission?

Sensitive actions require your approval before they're sent, scheduled, or shared.

Does it replace employees or office managers?

No. It's built to handle repetitive coordination and administrative work, so people can focus on judgment, relationships, and higher-value tasks.

Do I need technical experience?

No. The experience is designed for ordinary employees, with guided setup and simple everyday interactions.

Which workplace tools will it support?

The first version will prioritize email, calendar, documents, meetings, and task-management platforms based on early-user demand.

When will it be available?

Early users will be invited in stages. Joining the waiting list gives you priority access and product updates.
